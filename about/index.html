ABOUT RISE GROUP

A place for every child to rise

We bring learning, movement and creativity together so children can discover their strengths, build confidence and reach their full potential.

EXPLORE PROGRAMSVISIT RISE

OUR PURPOSE

Education should open more than one door

RISE Group was created to inspire and nurture individuals through high-quality education and meaningful learning experiences. Our student-centred teaching team helps children develop knowledge and practical skills while making room for curiosity, enjoyment and serious fun.

We believe a balanced childhood includes academic confidence, physical wellbeing and creative expression. That is why tutoring, sport, music, dance and visual arts live together within one connected community.

ONE COMMUNITY, THREE PATHWAYS

Learn. Move. Create.

Children grow in different ways. Our three academies give every student more ways to find what makes them thrive.

LEARN

Build strong foundations

Student-centred academic programs strengthen knowledge, confidence and independent thinking through engaging, structured learning.

MOVE

Grow through movement

Our sports programs turn energy into focus, coordination and resilience, with pathways from first participation to competition.

CREATE

Make ideas visible

Art, dance and music give students space to discover their voice, practise with purpose and share their work through exhibitions and performance.

THE RISE EXPERIENCE

High standards, human connection

Our educators and coaches create purposeful lessons while recognising the person behind every learner. Students are encouraged to practise, ask questions, try again, perform, compete and celebrate one another.

A sense of belonging

Every student is welcomed, known and encouraged to participate.

Respectful by design

Safe, supportive environments allow children to learn with confidence.

Celebrate progress

We recognise effort, improvement and achievement at every level.
