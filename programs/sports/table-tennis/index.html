COURSE INTRODUCTION

From first rally to competition play

Rise Table Tennis develops footwork, stroke technique and match strategy across beginner, small-group and competition classes. Coaches tailor drills to each student's level, with dedicated pathways for junior and adult competitors.

Small group coaching
Junior & adult squads

SUITABLE AGES
Ages 6+
Beginner Class

No experience required — grip, footwork and basic strokes in a fun, low-pressure setting.

Ages 8+
Small Group Improvement (1:4)

Focused coaching in groups of four, refining technique and consistency.

Ages 7+
Group Foundation Training

Structured group drills building rally consistency and match basics.

Ages 8–17
Junior Competition Squad

Competitive training and match play for junior players preparing for tournaments.

Ages 18+
Adult Competition Squad

High-intensity training and match play for competitive adult players.
