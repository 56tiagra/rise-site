Profiles from the 2024 RISE information booklet

Cyprian Koffsovitz

UKULELE & CONTEMPORARY MUSIC

Cyprian holds an Advanced Diploma in Music Performance from Melbourne Polytechnic, majoring in ukulele, and a Bachelor of Arts in Performance Studies from Victoria University. He performs at music and ukulele festivals around Australia.

Paul McCarthy

PIANO & MULTI-INSTRUMENTAL MUSIC

A classical pianist, multi-instrumentalist, vocalist, arranger and composer with extensive school teaching and music-directing experience. Paul's recorded piano work has featured on ABC Classic FM and 3MBS.
