COURSE INTRODUCTION

A creative path, level by level

Our Visual Arts program builds observation, technique and creative confidence across drawing, painting, sketching and design. Classes are grouped by age and skill level, so students are always working at the right level of challenge.

Small class sizes

SUITABLE AGES
Age 6
Creative Art

Open-ended, playful projects that build fine motor skills and creative confidence.

Age 8
Beginner Comprehensive Art

Introduces core techniques across drawing, painting and colour theory.

Age 10
Intermediate Comprehensive Art

Builds on foundational skills with more complex composition and media.

Age 12
Advanced Comprehensive Art

Develops a personal style and portfolio-ready pieces.

Ages 10+
Sketching

Focused study of line, form, light and shadow.

Ages 12+
Design

Introduces design thinking, layout and digital or graphic design fundamentals.

MEET THE TEAM

Teachers who inspire

Profiles from the 2024 RISE information booklet

Mihaela Banga

FINE ART TEACHER

A Romanian-born professional artist and Victorian-registered secondary art teacher whose work spans realism, contemporary, abstract, painterly and impressionist styles. Her work has been exhibited internationally, and she has taught children and adults across Europe and Australia.

Debra Stam

DRAWING & DESIGN TEACHER

Debra brings experience in secondary teaching, architectural drafting, interior and retail design, and TAFE education. Her drawing-led career gives students an applied understanding of line, colour, finish and design.

Irene Diaz Cebrian

FINE ART & CREATIVE PRACTICE

A Barcelona-born multidisciplinary artist with a Fine Arts degree from the University of Barcelona. Irene also studied interior design, painting techniques, leather and jewellery, and has taught in schools and cultural centres.
